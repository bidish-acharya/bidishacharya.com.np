## Challenge

As the first in-house designer at an early-stage AI startup, the product had no established design language, and enterprise users needed dense skill-development workflows to feel simple rather than overwhelming.

## Approach

Led discovery and user research through to high-fidelity Figma delivery, designing course and activity creation flows and competency dashboards, then worked directly with engineering to implement refinements in the TypeScript/Vue.js frontend — including a full website redesign for clarity and speed.

## Outcome

Delivered a coherent design foundation for the platform and lifted the company site's GTmetrix performance score from 57 to 94.
