## Challenge

Delivering consistent learning experiences across mobile, web, offline, and television — for a userbase in the millions — required design and delivery to move in lockstep at speed.

## Approach

Led end-to-end product design and frontend development while acting as product owner: writing user stories, managing the backlog, and planning sprints across design, development, and business stakeholders.

## Outcome

Shipped 10+ web projects hands-on and sustained a platform serving more than 3 million users across four distinct surfaces.
