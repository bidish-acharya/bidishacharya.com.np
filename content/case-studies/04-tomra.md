## Challenge

Users frequently opened support tickets for issues that documentation should have resolved, pointing to gaps in the product's self-service experience.

## Approach

Designed and prototyped a dedicated Help Section, using Google Analytics alongside Hotjar heatmaps and session recordings to locate real workflow bottlenecks and target the content and structure of the redesign.

## Outcome

Iterations grounded in behavioral data improved user clarity and reduced reliance on direct support requests.
