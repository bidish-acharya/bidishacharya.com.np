## Challenge

Nepal's international cricket history lacked a single, trustworthy analytics home — data was scattered, inconsistently recorded, and unusable for fans or analysts wanting real depth.

## Approach

Designed the product and data model end to end, then built a React, Vite, Tailwind, and Recharts frontend on a Python/pandas pipeline — cross-validating incomplete historical data against established sources while shaping the interface around what the numbers could actually support.

## Outcome

Shipped a data-rich platform now iterated on based on community feedback and usage analytics, with a data pipeline resilient enough to keep expanding into new formats and eras.
