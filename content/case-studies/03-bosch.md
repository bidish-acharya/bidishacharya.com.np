## Challenge

The Bosch DIY app, used by over a million people monthly, had accumulated navigation friction and unclear task flows that large-scale user feedback consistently flagged.

## Approach

Ran a heuristic evaluation across core flows, then redesigned the navigation hierarchy, added progress indicators, and refined UX copy — while extending the shared component library across the DIY e-commerce experience on mobile, tablet, and desktop, and designing data-driven Backoffice dashboards for internal teams.

## Outcome

App store rating improved from 4.24 to 4.41, alongside a more consistent, accessible interaction pattern across platforms.
